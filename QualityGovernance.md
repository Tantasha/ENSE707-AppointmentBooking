# Process Assurance VS Product Assurance

| Area | Process Assurance | Product Assurance |
|:--- :|:---:|:---:|
| Main focus | How the work is performed | Quality of the software product |
| Example in this project | Requirements review, coding standards, Git commits, test process| Validation logic, working booking feature, passing tests |
| Evidence | Review checklist, commits, test plan, CI results | Test results, defect reports, working prototype |
| Goal | Prevent quality problems | Detect and confirm product quality |

Why is both Process Assurance and Product Assurance needed?

Process Assurance ensures that the development process is followed correctly, which helps prevent defects and issues from arising in the first place. It focuses on the methods, practices, and standards used during development to ensure consistency and reliability.
While, Product Assurance focuses on the end result, ensuring that the final product meets the required quality standards and functions as intended. It involves testing, validation, and verification of the software to confirm that it meets user needs and specifications.
